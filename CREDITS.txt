barrels, ribbed focus ring, optical elements, aperture, and rear mount.
It is an illustrative optical assembly, not a model of a specific product,
and is not presented as Sriakshay's own modeling work.

sequence on https://animejs.com/; no Anime.js site models or art were copied.
